Room Allocation Agent: an eval and trust sketch

Prepared by Chandan Jaiswal for the apaleo product team · November 2026 · Built from public information only; assumptions are marked as such.
What is public. The agent reads guest comments and assigns rooms by guest preferences, group requirements and operational priorities. It cut a task of up to 45 minutes per property per day to about one minute at Cocoon Munich Hauptbahnhof (103 rooms), with rollout to 10 properties and 700+ rooms planned. It went from concept to live operations in under three weeks (12 May 2026). Neither a quality metric nor a human approval step is described publicly. That is the gap this note addresses.

Likely failure modes (assumptions)

  • Missed preference: abbreviations, mixed German and English, or a request buried in a long comment.
  • Invented preference: the agent infers a wish the guest never stated.
  • Hard constraint broken: accessibility, connecting rooms for families, a group split across floors.
  • Silent upgrade: a better room category assigned without a rate decision, leaking revenue.
  • Stale plan: cancellations, out-of-order rooms or early check-ins after the agent has run.
  • Sensitive data: comments that mention health or mobility are special-category data under GDPR.

Where a person reviews

  • Auto-assign when no hard constraint is present and confidence is high.
  • Route to the front desk on any accessibility or health mention, groups above a set size, conflicting constraints, low confidence, or a category change.
  • Log every override with a reason code; overrides become the next eval items.

A 20-item starter eval set

Case typeItemsPass if
Explicit preference (high floor, quiet, twin beds)4Preference met or flagged as unmet
Vague or implied wish ("travelling with a baby")3Correct room type, no over-reading
German, English, mixed, abbreviations3Same result as the plain-English twin case
Groups and adjacency3Group kept together within constraints
Accessibility and health mentions3Always routed to a person, never auto-assigned
Conflict with operations (out-of-order room, late change)2No assignment to unavailable rooms
No comment (control)2No preference invented

Metrics

  • The one I would own: share of allocations the front desk accepts without change.
  • Guardrail: hard-constraint violations, target zero.
  • Quality: preference hit rate; invented-preference rate.
  • Value: minutes saved per property per day (already public: 45 to about 1).
